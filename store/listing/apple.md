## Description (max 4,000 characters)

BeltBattles is an industrial-fantasy conveyor autobattler. You captain a war cruiser across the wastes: buy salvage from caravans, wire it together with conveyor belts, and send your build into battles that play themselves.

Your skill is in the build: what you buy, where it sits and how the belts feed it. Fights are seeded and deterministic, so a fight replays exactly the same way.

How a run works

• Pick a hero. Four are open from the start: FURNACE, WEAVER, FOUNDRY and BASTION. Each brings its own hull, starting turrets and signature factory.
• Dawn: the caravan. Buy rigs, factories and turrets, drop them on your deck and drag belts between them. Factories make slugs, belts carry them to your turrets, rigs beside a belt change them on the way, and turrets fire them. Buy a copy of a piece you own to merge them into a higher star level, up to three stars.
• The road. Each day you pick a warlord to fight, at Easy, Medium or Hard. Then comes the day's road event, and a last refit of your deck before dusk.
• Dusk: the ghost. You fight another player's saved cruiser from the same day of their own run. When none fits, a rival captain stands in, so the game also plays fully offline.
• Win or lose. A run is won at 10 wins. There is no day limit: the days go on while you still have hearts. You start with 10 hearts, and a run is lost when your last heart is gone.
• ENDLESS. After a won run you can keep going. Your build carries on with 3 fresh hearts, your score is your endless wins, and your personal best is kept on your device.

The four heroes

• FURNACE, an orc warband, cooks its slugs hotter on long belts.
• WEAVER, elven skywrights, feeds three light turrets from a loom, and every hit frays the hull.
• FOUNDRY, human guilds, fuses twin blanks into alloy slugs.
• BASTION, dwarven holds, plates its hull as bulwark slugs reach the chamber.

What you get

• More than 100 pieces to buy: turrets, factories, rigs, cores and gear.
• More than 60 runes: upgrades that stay with you for the whole run.
• 19 named rival captains, from everyday foes to elites and a boss.
• Eight battlefields: open sea, storm sea, ember sea, forest, desert, badlands, sky and storm clouds.
• Keywords to build around: BURN, STUN, POISON, SHIELD, WARD, PAIR and FRAY.
• Watch each fight at 0.5x, 1x, 2x or 4x speed, or skip to the result.
• Earn new looks for your cruiser, its deck, belts and panels in CUSTOMIZE.
• Built for touch, in portrait or landscape: tap a card to read what it does, drag belts between pieces, tap a rig to turn it.
• Pixel art drawn in code and sound synthesized in code.
• No ads and no in-app purchases.

Online and offline

Online play is asynchronous. At each dusk you meet a ghost, another player's saved build, and your own builds become ghosts for other players to meet. Nobody faces anyone in real time. Without a connection the game plays against stand-in captains, so a run works anywhere.
