How to reach us

Linking a second device

  1. On the first device open OPTIONS, then LINK A DEVICE, then SHOW MY CODE. The game shows a code like XXXX-XXXX and the minutes left. It works once, for 10 minutes.
  2. On the second device open OPTIONS, then LINK A DEVICE, then ENTER A CODE. Type the code and tap OK.
  3. The second device says DEVICE LINKED. IT STARTS AT YOUR NEXT NEW RUN. From your next new run both devices play as one player. The second device also receives your online profile (your unlocked looks and progress counters) and begins to receive your run history.

Both devices must be online. THAT CODE DID NOT WORK. means the code was wrong, too old or already used: ask for a new one with NEW CODE. THIS DEVICE ALREADY HAS A LINKED PLAYER. means this device is already linked to another player, or has other devices linked to it, so it cannot join one.

Lost progress, a new phone or cleared data

Your game is saved on your device. If you delete the app, clear its data or move to a new phone, the saved game stays behind, unless your phone's backup restores it (privacy policy, section 9).

Your online profile and your run history live on the server under your player ID. To bring them to a new device, link the new device to the old one with the steps above while you still have the old one. A run in progress does not move.

The game has no account, so without the old device a link is not possible: the new device starts as a new player. If you want us to delete the old online data, see the privacy policy, section 7.

Playing offline

BeltBattles plays without a connection. Your opponents are then stand-in captains, the title screen says OFFLINE: PLAYING AGAINST STAND-INS, and nothing is sent. When your connection is back, the game goes back to meeting other players' ghosts.

Deleting your data

In the game open OPTIONS, then DELETE MY ONLINE DATA. The steps, what is removed and what to do if you can no longer use the game are in the privacy policy, section 7.

Privacy policy

The privacy policy says what the game stores on your device, what it sends to its server, who sees it and how long it is kept.
